Social Media Brand Guidelines: Structuring a High-Authority Instagram & LinkedIn Feed

How to standardize your 1080×1350 portrait grid, build educational carousels, and create an instantly recognizable visual signature.

Your social profile is often a prospect's first impression of your company—even before they click your website link. Within three seconds of landing on your grid, visitors judge whether your brand feels credible or chaotic.

Key Building Blocks of a Cohesive Social System

1. Standardize on 4:5 Portrait (1080×1350)

Square posts leave screen real estate on the table. The 1080×1350 vertical format commands 25% more mobile screen space, lifting dwell time and engagement.

2. Balanced Content Pillars

Rotate cleanly between promotional offers, swipeable educational carousels, and social proof or behind-the-scenes brand stories.

3. Disciplined 3-Color Hierarchy

Anchor your feed with one primary background tone, one clean contrast color for body text, and one signature accent (like gold or emerald) for key hooks and CTAs.
